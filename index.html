Our Story

A small kitchen with a big sweet tooth

Elite Cakes started as a passion for baking for family and friends. Today, we bake for celebrations all over Vavuniya, but we still treat every order like it's for our own family.

Every cake is made from scratch, to order, never sitting on a shelf. That means fresh sponge, real cream and a design made just for you.
